The Wilderness Expedition.

Deep off-grid, with no exits and no signal. A river run, an alpine trek, open water — multi-day expeditions where a leadership team has no choice but to become one crew, reading the conditions and moving together.

Why it works

No exits. No signal. One crew.

A multi-day wilderness expedition removes the two things a stressed team hides behind: the off-ramp and the phone. Three days down a river, or a trek deep into the backcountry, and the team has to read conditions, make calls, and rely on each other in real time. There is no stepping out to take a meeting. The shared journey becomes the story they tell for years.

River, alpine, or open water — guided, and as remote as the outcome calls for. This is a format, not the product. If it serves your function, we run it; if a setting closer to home serves it better, we'll tell you.

Straight answers

What is a wilderness expedition retreat?

A multi-day guided journey by river, alpine trail or open water where a leadership team has no signal and no exits, and has to work as one crew.

Do we need outdoor experience?

No. Everything is guided and paced to the team; moderate fitness is enough.

Is there any connection to the office?

No, by design. We carry satellite communication for safety only.

How much does a wilderness expedition retreat cost?

Usually a Signature retreat, $45,000 to $150,000, or Bespoke for longer or more remote routes.
