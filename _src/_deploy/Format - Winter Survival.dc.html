Signature format · Winter Survival

The Winter Survival retreat.

Cold, real stakes, and a team that has to work as one to be comfortable. When the conditions make interdependence non-negotiable, the stubborn divides and egos a team carries simply melt away.

Why it works

When the cold is real, the pretense stops.

There's no posturing at twenty below. Building a snow shelter, managing warmth and energy, depending on the person next to you — winter conditions create a clean, shared challenge where the only way to be okay is together. The lessons that surface (pace yourself, protect your people, don't burn out the strongest) map directly onto how the team operates back home.

Snow caves, alpine expeditions, dog sledding — all run with expert guides and real safety. This is a format, not the product. If it serves your outcome, we run it; if a gentler setting serves it better, we'll tell you.

Best for
Breaking divides

Teams split into camps who need a reason to depend on one another.

Ego & overwork

When the strongest are burning out and no one's pulling them back.

Hardiness

Proving to a team, viscerally, that it can do hard things together.
