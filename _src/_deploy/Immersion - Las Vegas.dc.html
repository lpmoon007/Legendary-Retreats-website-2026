Your team is already in Las Vegas for the conference. Give them one day that has nothing to do with it: a guided off-road day in the Mojave, built around the outcome you need, with the debrief that turns it into how they work. Breakfast departure, back for dinner.

Why Las Vegas

The easiest place in the country to steal a day.

Most Las Vegas team building happens indoors, a few floors below the ballroom. Thirty to seventy-five minutes from the Strip there is red sandstone, sandy washes and slickrock across the Nellis badlands, the Nelson Hills and Eldorado Canyon, with the 45,000-acre Logandale trail system when a longer transfer earns it. Two executives per Jeep, a professional guide in the lead, and a day designed so the terrain does the teaching.

Because the flights, lodging and meeting space are already in place, the day costs your team one calendar slot and no extra travel. If what you need is a full retreat rather than a day, Death Valley is about two hours away.

Planning a Las Vegas day

One day: a breakfast departure and a dinner return. Teams that want more can add Death Valley or a multi-day expedition.
