Planning a retreat in Jackson Hole

The practical details.

When to go

June to September and December to March. Many lodges close in the spring and fall shoulder seasons.

Getting there

Jackson Hole Airport (JAC), inside Grand Teton National Park, is about 15 minutes from town, with seasonal nonstops from major hubs. Private aviation is well served.

How long

Three days. Flights in and out favor midday arrivals and departures.

Where you stay

A ranch lodge with Teton views and private space for the conversations that matter.

A sample three-day shape

A starting point. Every retreat is built around your one outcome.

Day 1

Arrive

Midday into JAC, a short transfer, and an evening with the Tetons and the one question.

Day 2

Perspective

A morning of hard conversation; an afternoon on horseback, the river or the snow.

Day 3

Decide

Decisions and one behavior per leader, then out on afternoon flights.

Straight answers

When is the best time for a Jackson Hole retreat?

Summer (June to September) or winter (December to March). Many lodges close in the shoulder seasons.

How do we get to Jackson Hole?

Jackson Hole Airport (JAC) is about 15 minutes from town and has seasonal nonstops from major hubs; private aviation is well served.

Is Jackson Hole good for a board retreat?

Yes. It is one of our strongest settings for board and CEO-team offsites that want awe and privacy without giving up comfort.

How long should we plan for?

Three days, built around midday flights.

Can you run a retreat in Jackson Hole for a leadership team of 8 to 12?

Yes. Retreats in Jackson Hole are private and designed for leadership teams of roughly 5 to 12. Where you stay: a ranch lodge with Teton views and private space for the conversations that matter. Larger groups are possible with a different format; we will tell you honestly what changes.

What is included in a retreat in Jackson Hole?

Design, facilitation by James Carter, lodging, meals, activities, on-site coordination and ground transfers, plus a 30-day challenge with a written day-30 report. Airfare is the usual exclusion. Focused and Signature retreats come with a written proposal within 72 hours.

Can a Jackson Hole retreat include Grand Teton or Yellowstone?

Yes. Grand Teton National Park surrounds the valley, and the airport sits inside it. In summer, Yellowstone’s South Entrance is roughly an hour and a quarter north of Jackson. A guided day in either park can be built into the retreat when it serves the outcome.

Can you run a winter retreat in Jackson Hole?

Yes. December to March, with Jackson Hole Mountain Resort about half an hour from town and backcountry suited to snow and winter-survival formats. Skiing is optional; the retreat is built around one outcome.

Who a retreat in Jackson Hole is for

Private retreats for CEOs and leadership teams of roughly 5 to 12, built around one outcome. We are strongest with teams that need to work as one: teams in conflict, split into camps, or newly merged. Every retreat is turnkey, and Focused and Signature retreats come with a written proposal within 72 hours.
