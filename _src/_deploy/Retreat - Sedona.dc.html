Planning a retreat in Sedona

The practical details.

When to go

March to May and October to November are ideal. Summer is hot, with afternoon monsoon storms from July to September; winter is mild with the occasional snow.

Getting there

Phoenix Sky Harbor (PHX) is about 2 hours by road; Flagstaff (FLG) about 45 minutes. Sedona has a small airport for private aircraft.

How long

Two to three days. Sedona is built for thinking time, so the agenda leaves room to breathe.

Where you stay

One of the red-rock resorts, with private meeting space and terraces built for long conversations.

A sample three-day shape

A starting point. Every retreat is built around your one outcome.

Day 1

Transfer from Phoenix, settle in, and an evening framing the one outcome over dinner.

Straight answers

When is the best time for a Sedona retreat?

Spring (March to May) and fall (October to November). Summer brings heat and afternoon monsoon storms; winter is mild and quieter.

How far is Sedona from the airport?

About two hours by road from Phoenix Sky Harbor, or about 45 minutes from Flagstaff. Small private aircraft can use Sedona's own airport.

Is Sedona only for relaxed retreats?

No. It suits strategy resets and realignment, and the red-rock terrain also supports an off-road day when the outcome calls for shared challenge.

How long should a Sedona offsite be?

Two to three days for most leadership teams.
