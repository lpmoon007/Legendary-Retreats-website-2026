Planning a retreat in the Sierra Nevada

The practical details.

When to go

June to October for granite trails, high lakes and the Rubicon; December to March for snow and winter-survival formats.

Getting there

Reno-Tahoe (RNO) and Sacramento (SMF) serve the northern Sierra; Fresno (FAT) the Yosemite side. Truckee (TRK) handles private aircraft.

How long

Three days for most formats; four when the team needs both a hard day and a reflective one.

Where you stay

Five-star mountain lodges a short drive from the backcountry, or a remote cabin when closeness is the point.

A sample three-day shape

A starting point. Every retreat is built around your one outcome.

Day 1

Arrive

Transfer from Reno or Sacramento and an evening framing the outcome.

Day 2

The range

Winter: a guided snow-shelter or ridge day. Summer: a granite trail or the Rubicon by four-wheel drive.

Day 3

Decide

A morning in the lodge to turn the day into decisions and one behavior per leader.

Straight answers

When is the best season for a Sierra Nevada retreat?

June to October for summer terrain, December to March for snow formats.

How do we get to the Sierra Nevada?

Reno-Tahoe or Sacramento for the northern Sierra, Fresno for the Yosemite side; Truckee for private aircraft.

Can the same retreat be both demanding and restful?

Yes. The Sierra lets us pair a hard day in the backcountry with reflective work in a five-star lodge a few miles away.

Do we need mountaineering experience?

No. Every day is guided and designed around the team you have.

Can you run a retreat in the Sierra Nevada for a leadership team of 8 to 12?

Yes. Retreats in the Sierra Nevada are private and designed for leadership teams of roughly 5 to 12. Where you stay: five-star mountain lodges a short drive from the backcountry, or a remote cabin when closeness is the point. Larger groups are possible with a different format; we will tell you honestly what changes.

What is included in a retreat in the Sierra Nevada?

Design, facilitation by James Carter, lodging, meals, activities, on-site coordination and ground transfers, plus a 30-day challenge with a written day-30 report. Airfare is the usual exclusion. Focused and Signature retreats come with a written proposal within 72 hours.

Who a retreat in the Sierra Nevada is for

Private retreats for CEOs and leadership teams of roughly 5 to 12, built around one outcome. We are strongest with teams that need to work as one: teams in conflict, split into camps, or newly merged. Every retreat is turnkey, and Focused and Signature retreats come with a written proposal within 72 hours.
