The Complete Retreat Planning Kit

Most retreats fail for the same reason: they’re built as events — fill the days, book the venue, hope people bond. The good ones are built as decisions. This kit is organized around that difference — the principles, the agenda shape, the checklist, and the follow-through that make an executive retreat change something.

  • 03 The agenda shape The ⅓ content / ⅓ conversation / ⅓ connection rule — and how to build the day around it.
  • 04 Venue & logistics checklist Everything that quietly makes or breaks the room, in one list.
  • 05 The countdown A prep timeline — what to lock, and when, from weeks out to the day of.
  • 06 Budget worksheet A simple frame for what to spend where, without guessing.
  • 07 The 30-day follow-through The retreat isn’t the point. What happens after is — here’s how to hold it.
