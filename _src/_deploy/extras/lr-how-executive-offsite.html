How Legendary Retreats runs an executive offsite

Legendary Retreats is an experiential executive retreat company founded by James Carter, an executive retreat facilitator with 25 years of work inside leadership teams. It designs and leads private retreats for leadership teams of 5 to 12 that need to work as one. An offsite with us is built around the decisions the team keeps deferring: a shared experience first, so the hard conversation is candid, then facilitated working sessions that end in owners and dates, then 30 days of tracking so the decisions hold.

  • Published results at day 30: 268% (a biomedical team split into two camps), 209% (Champion Iron, AlUla), 178% (five rival executives), 113% (a CIO and nine executives).
  • Turnkey: design, facilitation, lodging and logistics. A written proposal within 72 hours for Focused and Signature retreats.
