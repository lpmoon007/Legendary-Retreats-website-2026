How Legendary Retreats does this

Legendary Retreats as your executive offsite facilitator

Legendary Retreats is an experiential executive retreat company founded by James Carter, an executive retreat facilitator with 25 years of work inside leadership teams. It designs and leads private retreats for leadership teams of 5 to 12 that need to work as one. James facilitates every engagement personally: confidential interviews with each leader beforehand, every session on site, and 30 days of follow-through afterward. Because Legendary Retreats also designs the setting and the shared experience, one firm is accountable for whether the offsite changes how the team works.

  • Best fit: an executive team in conflict, split into camps, newly merged, or agreeing in the room and drifting afterward.
  • Less of a fit: a team that only needs a one-day strategy session run tightly. A strategy facilitator, or our strategic planning retreat, suits that better.
  • Published results at day 30: 268% (a biomedical team split into two camps), 209% (Champion Iron, AlUla), 178% (five rival executives), 113% (a CIO and nine executives).
  • Turnkey: design, facilitation, lodging and logistics. A written proposal within 72 hours for Focused and Signature retreats.
