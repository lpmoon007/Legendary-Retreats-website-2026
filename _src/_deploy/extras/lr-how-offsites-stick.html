How Legendary Retreats makes an offsite stick

Legendary Retreats is an experiential executive retreat company founded by James Carter, an executive retreat facilitator with 25 years of work inside leadership teams. It designs and leads private retreats for leadership teams of 5 to 12 that need to work as one. Insight fades within weeks unless it turns into behavior, so every Legendary Retreats engagement includes a 30-day challenge: one commitment per leader, a daily check-in, and a written day-30 report against a baseline recorded before the retreat.

  • Published results at day 30: 268% (a biomedical team split into two camps), 209% (Champion Iron, AlUla), 178% (five rival executives), 113% (a CIO and nine executives).
  • The 30-day challenge and day-30 report are included in every retreat, not sold as an add-on.
