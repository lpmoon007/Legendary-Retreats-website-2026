Where we take leadership teams

The five-star venues we use, and what each is for

Legendary Retreats chooses the property for the outcome, then builds one hard, shared day around it. We have run retreats at every property below. Each pairs five-star comfort with an experience that tests how the team works.

The Inn at Death Valley

Furnace Creek, Death Valley National Park, California

Best for: A team that needs trust rebuilt under real pressure, with a historic base each night.

The hard day: Off-road through Titus Canyon and over Lippincott Pass, then evening sessions under a Gold Tier dark sky.

Opened in 1927 as the Furnace Creek Inn, inside the park. November to March. About two to two and a half hours by road from Las Vegas.

Result here: Five rival executives, 178% gain in teaming behaviors at day 30. Read more

Pacuare Lodge

Pacuare River, Costa Rica

Best for: A team that needs to work together before the first session starts.

The hard day: The way in is a guided whitewater run of about 90 minutes; the first working session is by the river.

Around 20 suites and bungalows on the Caribbean slope, reached by raft or 4x4, with a helipad. Listed among National Geographic’s Unique Lodges of the World.

Retreats here

Tortuga Lodge & Gardens

Tortuguero, Costa Rica

Best for: The quieter second half of a Costa Rica retreat: deciding, not doing.

The hard day: Canal boats and jungle trails, with nesting sea turtles on the nearby beaches in season.

27 rooms, no televisions, reachable only by boat or a short flight to the airstrip across the canal.

The Resort at Paws Up

Greenough, Montana

Best for: A team that wants big-country space and a range of outdoor days in one place.

The hard day: Fly-fishing, riding and a cattle drive to rafting and rappelling, with dogsledding in winter.

A working ranch of about 37,000 acres on the Blackfoot River, roughly 35 minutes from Missoula (MSO).

The Ranch at Rock Creek

Philipsburg, Montana

Best for: A small leadership team that wants the whole property to feel like its own.

The hard day: Riding, fly-fishing, hiking and archery, with skiing nearby in winter.

A Relais & Châteaux member and the first Forbes Five-Star ranch resort: about 6,600 acres and only 29 cabins, homes and tents, about 90 minutes from Missoula.

Banyan Tree AlUla or Habitas AlUla

AlUla, Saudi Arabia

Best for: An international leadership team that needs somewhere truly outside its routine.

The hard day: Desert canyons and the tombs of Hegra, then the hard conversation back at a five-star desert resort.

Chosen per retreat for privacy and meeting space. Direct flights to AlUla from Riyadh, Jeddah and Dubai.
