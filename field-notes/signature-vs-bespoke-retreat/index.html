Signature vs. bespoke: which retreat is right for your team?

Both are fully facilitated, built around a real outcome, and reinforced for 30 days. The only real difference is how much is designed from scratch. A Signature retreat is ready to run; a Bespoke retreat is built from nothing around your team. Here's how to tell which one you need.

Signature
Ready to run
$5K–$12K / person
  • Proven location & activity arc
  • Tailored content core
  • Faster to launch, lighter lift
  • Named formats (Cabin Fever, Off-Road…)
Bespoke
Built from scratch
$75K–$500K+ turnkey
  • Engineered to one exact outcome
  • Everything custom-designed
  • Pre-work to scope before travel
  • For high, precise stakes

Choose Signature when…

  • ✓You want a genuinely great retreat without a long design runway.
  • ✓Your timeline or budget is tighter, and you'd rather move now.
  • ✓The outcome is real but not unusual — trust, alignment, reset, momentum.

Choose Bespoke when…

  • ✦The stakes are high and exact — the retreat has to land one specific outcome.
  • ✦The situation is unusual or sensitive, and a stock format won't reach it.
  • ✦The experience itself should be one-of-a-kind and unforgettable.

Not sure? That's the first conversation. We'll tell you honestly which fits — and sometimes the right answer is the simpler, less expensive one. The goal is the outcome, not the invoice.
