01

Who these are for

Our retreats are designed for CEOs and senior leadership teams of 5–12 — most often companies scaling through real complexity, where the cost of a misaligned executive team is measured in stalled decisions and missed quarters. If your team is talented but pulling in slightly different directions, this is built for you.

02 · The method

It starts with a question — not a location.

Before terrain, lodging, or activities, we get clear on the one thing that matters: what you need your team to walk away with. We ask you two questions.

Question 01

"What do you want your people walking away feeling, thinking, and saying out loud to one another?"

Question 02

"If you could achieve only one thing on this retreat, what would it be?"

Answer those and we've found your function — the outcome the retreat exists to create. Everything else is engineered in service of it.

03 · The craft

Format follows function.

Once we know the function, we engineer the format around it — the environment, the activities, the conversations, all pointed at your one outcome. You set the preferences: five-star or rugged, remote or reachable, no beach, no bugs. We design around all of it.

A format is never chosen because it photographs well — sometimes it's chosen deliberately against expectation, because that's what moves your team.

Formats are just tools — a few we've used
Off-road immersion Open-water sailing Jungle trek Remote lodge & "cabin fever" Five-star estate Horseback & ranch High-alpine expedition 1800s mining lodge
One example Emotion is motion

For one team, the right move was the opposite of luxury — they expected five-star and golf; we booked an 1800s mining lodge. Shared baths, cots, closet rooms.

The moment they arrived, their emotion shifted — and emotion is motion. Your retreat might be the exact opposite: serene, five-star, remote. The point is never the lodge. It's the deliberate design behind it.

04

From A, to B, to lasting change

Most executive retreats create temporary inspiration. We build the measurement in — so you can see the change, not just hope for it.

STEP 01
Your baseline

Together we name the 3–5 behaviors costing your team most right now, and set a baseline to measure against.

STEP 02
Your immersion

A retreat engineered to your outcome — environment, activities, and conversations all pointed at it. Never off a menu.

STEP 03
30-day reinforcement

Daily accountability prompts, behavioral tracking, micro-journaling, and facilitator re-engagement.

STEP 04
Measured ROI

A behavioral-change report measuring movement against the baseline. Proof, not hope.
