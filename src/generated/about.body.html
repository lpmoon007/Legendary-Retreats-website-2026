Meet James Carter.

You don't need another facilitator with a binder. You need someone who has sat where your hardest calls get made — and knows how to get a team to the other side of them. For 25 years, that's been James's work.

In the room for the decisions that matter

When your team is stuck, the last thing that helps is an outsider with a curriculum. What helps is someone who has sat inside executive teams — in the room as they made the calls that defined their companies — and knows how to surface what's unspoken and get a team through it. That's the vantage point James works from: not delivering a program, but reading your room.

He's co-authored books alongside Stephen Covey, Ken Blanchard, Deepak Chopra, and Brian Tracy, and his work has been featured by CNN, CNN Money, and Business Insider. But the credential that matters most isn't on a cover or a broadcast — it's the thousands of leaders who left a retreat changed, and stayed that way.

The work is human

The breakthrough rarely happens in a session. It happens on the ground, over coffee, when the titles come off.

"Teams don't trust him because of the names on a book cover. They trust him because he gets them to the other side."

None of this is the point — your team's outcome is. It's just the reason you can hand James your hardest team problem and know he's seen worse, and gotten a team out of it.

A retreat that reaches further

Your team changes. So does a 12-year-old you'll never meet.

Be Legendary is built as a social enterprise. Every executive who attends sends a young person aged 12–15 into a leadership workshop of their own — at the age when who they'll become is still taking shape. They do the work, find the nerve, and lead for the first time. Your team makes it possible; we help them get there.

It's why the company exists, and why "legendary" was never only about the boardroom.
