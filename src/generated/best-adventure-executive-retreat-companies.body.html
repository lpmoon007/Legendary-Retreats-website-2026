Choosing an adventure retreat · 2026

The best adventure and wilderness executive retreat companies.

Six firms that use the outdoors to change how a leadership team works, from survival scenarios to multi-week expeditions. They differ most in who leads, how hard the experience is, and what happens after everyone flies home.

By James Carter · Updated October 2026
Our bias, up front. We are Legendary Retreats, and we are on this list. Each firm is described only by what it or published coverage says it does. Where we relied on third-party coverage rather than the firm's own site, we say so. If something is out of date, tell us and we will correct it.

Quick picks by what you need

  • A senior team in conflict, with five-star comfort at night: Legendary Retreats.
  • Proven outdoor team development through a regional school: Outward Bound Professional.
  • A real expedition, days to weeks in the backcountry: NOLS Professional Training.
  • A whole company or large group in a nature destination, logistics handled: Wilder or Adventure Architects.
  • Travel with a purpose-driven, cultural component: Discover Corps.

How we compared them

Adventure is a means, not the point. We looked at what a buyer of an executive retreat needs to know.

  • Who leadsA named principal, staff instructors, or your own facilitator.
  • How hardGuided outdoor activity, a scenario with real stakes, or a full expedition.
  • Group sizeBuilt for a 5-to-12-person leadership team or for larger groups.
  • AfterAny measurement or follow-through once the team is home.

The companies

Legendary Retreats legendary-retreats.com

Best for: executive teams that need to work as one, including teams in conflict

What they do
Designs and leads experiential retreats for CEOs and leadership teams of roughly 5 to 12: winter survival, off-road expeditions, sailing, wilderness scenarios and remote lodges, with five-star lodging so the hard day ends with comfort. See the formats and destinations.
Who leads
James Carter, the founder, on every retreat.

A 30-day challenge and written day-30 report. How it is measured.

Published: Focused $35,000 to $50,000, Signature $45,000 to $150,000, Bespoke $75,000 to $500,000+, turnkey, airfare usually excluded. Written proposal within 72 hours for Focused and Signature.
Not the right fit
Groups much larger than 20, or a single afternoon of team building.

Outward Bound Professional outwardbound.org

Best for: outdoor team and leadership development with a long track record

What they do
The professional arm of Outward Bound runs custom team and leadership programs through regional schools, from a single day to several days, with collaboration, communication and problem solving among the listed focus areas.
Worth knowing
Each regional school runs its own programs, so format and venues vary by location.
Ask them
Which regional school would deliver your program, and who would facilitate it.

NOLS Professional Training nols.edu

Best for: teams that want a genuine expedition

What they do
The professional training arm of the wilderness school NOLS, running since 1999. It offers one-to-three-day custom leadership courses and wilderness expeditions of roughly 3 to 30 days for organizations, including corporate teams.
Worth knowing
The experience is the expedition itself: backcountry travel and living, taught by NOLS instructors. Comfort is not part of the design.
Ask them
How the course is adapted for executives with limited time, and what follow-up is available.

Wilder discoverwilder.com

Best for: company retreats in nature destinations, with logistics handled

What they do
According to published coverage, Wilder designs and manages corporate retreats in nature-based destinations, mixing structured meetings with outdoor activity, in places such as Bend, Vail, Yosemite and Banff.
Worth knowing
Described in trade coverage as founded by Kirk Reynolds. We relied on third-party coverage for this entry; confirm current offerings directly.

Adventure Architects adventurearchitects.co

Best for: larger team retreats and events with an adventure component

What they do
Based in Victoria, Canada and South Lake Tahoe, California, per its marketplace profile. Designs and produces team experiences, from small group trips to multi-day company retreats; a published case study describes producing a five-day retreat in Palm Springs for the software company 15Five.

Positioned for company retreats and events more than for a single small executive team. Details are from its profile on The Vendry.

Discover Corps discovercorps.com

Best for: leadership travel with a purpose-driven, cultural component

What they do
Designs retreats that combine travel with leadership work, including leadership offsites and incentive trips, built around cultural immersion and time with local changemakers, with a travel specialist handling logistics.
Worth knowing
Closer to purposeful travel than to a hard-edged adventure; a good fit when the goal is perspective rather than resolving conflict.

Adventure retreat or adventure activity?

A zip line, rafting trip or ropes course is an activity. It can be fun and it can break the ice. An adventure retreat is different: the experience is designed around the outcome the team needs, debriefed while it is fresh, and tied to one change each leader will make back at work. If the provider cannot tell you what the team will do differently on day 30, you are buying an activity.

Questions buyers ask

What are the best adventure retreat companies for executive teams?

It depends on the goal. For a leadership team of 5 to 12 that needs to resolve conflict or rebuild trust, Legendary Retreats designs survival, off-road and wilderness scenarios with five-star lodging. Outward Bound Professional and NOLS Professional Training run outdoor team development and expeditions. Wilder and Adventure Architects plan nature-based company retreats for larger groups. Discover Corps offers purpose-driven leadership travel.

Do executives need to be fit or outdoorsy for an adventure retreat?

Not for a well-designed one. The challenge should be psychological and collaborative, not athletic. Ask the provider how the experience adapts to the least fit person on the team, and what the safety plan is.

Are wilderness retreats safe for executives?

With the right season, guides, vehicles, water planning and communications, yes. Ask any provider for its safety record, evacuation plan and guide credentials before booking.

How much does an adventure executive retreat cost?

Few providers publish prices. Legendary Retreats publishes turnkey tiers from $35,000 to $50,000 (Focused) up to $75,000 and beyond (Bespoke) for teams of about 5 to 12, airfare usually extra.
