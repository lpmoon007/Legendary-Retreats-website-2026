Choosing a retreat company · 2026

The best executive retreat companies, compared.

Eleven firms that run or plan executive and leadership retreats, grouped by what they actually do, with who each one is best for. "Executive retreat company" covers three different businesses, and most bad retreats start with hiring the wrong kind.

By James Carter · Updated October 2026
Our bias, up front. We are Legendary Retreats, and we are on this list. We have tried to describe every firm, including our own, only by what it publicly says it does, and to say plainly when another firm is the better fit. If something here is out of date, tell us and we will correct it.

Quick picks by what you need

  • A leadership team in conflict, or one that needs trust rebuilt: Legendary Retreats, or a custom Outward Bound Professional program.
  • A measured change in behavior after the retreat: Legendary Retreats (day-30 report included).
  • A large-organization leadership development program, not a single retreat: Center for Creative Leadership.
  • A team-building session with a charitable component: Odyssey Teams.
  • A strategic plan your team will execute: Leadership Strategies, Building Champions or the Lefko Group.
  • Venue, travel and logistics handled, with your own agenda: Offsite, Moniker Partners, Teamland or Simetras.

How we compared them

We judged each firm on what a buyer of an executive retreat actually needs to know, using only public information: the firm's own site and published profiles. Where we could not confirm something, we left it out.

  • What they ownLogistics, the sessions in the room, or the whole outcome.
  • Who leads the roomA named principal, a staff facilitator, or you.
  • FormatExperiential, facilitated working sessions, or event production.
  • After the retreatAny follow-through or measurement once the team is home.

Experiential retreats and team development

Firms that use shared experience, rather than a conference room, as the main tool. The right choice when the problem is trust, conflict or a team that is not yet a team.

Legendary Retreats legendary-retreats.com

Best for: executive teams that need to work as one, including teams in conflict

What they do
Designs and leads experiential retreats for CEOs and leadership teams of roughly 5 to 12: off-road, winter survival, sailing, wilderness and remote lodges, plus five-star strategic planning retreats. Turnkey: design, facilitation, lodging, meals, activities and transfers.
Who leads
James Carter, the founder, on every retreat.
After
A 30-day challenge with a written day-30 report against a baseline. How it is measured.
Pricing
Published: Focused $35,000 to $50,000, Signature $45,000 to $150,000, Bespoke $75,000 to $500,000+. Written proposal within 72 hours for Focused and Signature.
Not the right fit
Groups much larger than 20, logistics-only requests, or a single afternoon of team building.

Outward Bound Professional outwardbound.org

Best for: outdoor team and leadership development with a long track record

What they do
The professional arm of Outward Bound runs custom team and leadership programs through regional schools, from a single day to several days. Regional programs list collaboration, communication, problem solving and change management among their focus areas.
Worth knowing
Each regional school runs its own programs, so format and venues vary by location. The Chesapeake school cites more than 50 years of work with teams, including executive teams.
Ask them
Which regional school would deliver your program, and who would facilitate it.

Center for Creative Leadership ccl.org

Best for: larger organizations building leadership capability over months

What they do
A global leadership development institute offering custom programs for organizations, delivered in person and online, often as multi-session journeys with assessments and 360-degree feedback coaching.
Worth knowing
Strongest when the goal is a development program across many leaders rather than a single retreat for one team.

Odyssey Teams odysseyteams.com

Best for: established teams that want team building with a charitable outcome

What they do
Business-simulation team building with a philanthropic component, such as building bikes for children in need or assembling prosthetic hands. The company says it ran its first bike-building program in 1999, and it also offers virtual programs.
Worth knowing
A team-building program you add to an event, rather than a full retreat with venue and logistics.

Strategic planning facilitators and coaches

Firms that lead the working sessions. The right choice when the team is healthy and the job is a plan, and you are able to handle venue and logistics yourself or through a planner.

Building Champions buildingchampions.com

Best for: aligning an executive team on mission, strategy and priorities

What they do
An executive coaching firm that runs customized executive retreats, each led by one of its executive coaches. It describes two-day retreats aimed at a clear direction and an actionable 12 to 24 month plan.
Worth knowing
The firm says it has been coaching leaders for nearly 30 years. A natural fit if you also want ongoing executive coaching.

Leadership Strategies (LeadStrat) leadstrat.com

Best for: a structured strategic planning session

What they do
A facilitation firm offering strategic planning facilitation built on its Drivers Model, plus meeting facilitation and executive and team coaching.
Worth knowing
Planning methodology is the core product. Venue, travel and on-site logistics are typically separate.

The Lefko Group (Mark Lefko) marklefko.com

Best for: a CEO who wants an experienced peer-advisor to facilitate planning

What they do
Mark Lefko facilitates corporate and strategic planning retreats and CEO peer groups. His published bio says he has coached and mentored more than 100 CEOs, and he previously served as a group chair with Vistage Worldwide.
Worth knowing
Principal-led facilitation. Confirm what is included beyond the sessions themselves.

Logistics planners

Firms that source venues, handle contracts and travel, and run the event. The right choice when the agenda is already set and you, or a facilitator you hire, will lead the room.

Offsite offsite.com

Best for: team offsites where venue and logistics are the main job

What they do
A venue and vendor marketplace with negotiated rates, plus done-for-you planning: venue contracts, agendas, budgets, vendors and travel. Planning is priced as per-person fees across several plan tiers.
Worth knowing
Founded in 2020 and based in New York; raised $3 million in 2023 and reports hundreds of retreats in more than 15 countries. Not to be confused with similarly named firms.

Moniker Partners monikerpartners.com

Best for: travel logistics and on-site production for a company retreat

What they do
Combines travel logistics, experience design and on-site event execution, with a free proposal after an introductory call and a stated budget guarantee.
Ask them
Whether facilitation of the working sessions is included or needs a separate provider.

Teamland teamland.com

Best for: a packaged leadership offsite with optional coaching add-ons

What they do
Offers leadership offsites of 1 to 5 days for groups of 4 to 18, at a retreat venue or your location, with optional executive coaching, team assessments and quarterly alignment sessions. Also runs larger team offsites and team-building events.
Worth knowing
Handles vendor onboarding and procurement; asks for more lead time for retreats than for smaller events.

Simetras simetras.co

Best for: an offsite with leadership workshops built in

What they do
A Miami-based firm planning executive retreats, company offsites and outdoor or glamping retreats, with leadership workshops on trust, coaching and decision-making. Handles venue sourcing, travel and attendee management.

Who we left out, and why

Venues and resorts with in-house event teams, because they plan events at their own property rather than retreats. Large team-building marketplaces focused on activities rather than executive teams. And several firms we could not verify from public information. For what each type of company owns, see executive retreat planners: who does what, and for the facilitation side, how to choose an offsite facilitator.

Questions buyers ask

What is the best executive retreat company?

It depends on what the retreat has to do. For an experiential retreat that rebuilds trust or resolves conflict on a leadership team of 5 to 12, a full-service designer such as Legendary Retreats or a custom Outward Bound Professional program fits. For a strategic plan, a planning facilitator such as Leadership Strategies or a coaching firm such as Building Champions fits. If you only need venues, travel and logistics handled, a planner such as Offsite, Moniker Partners or Teamland fits.

What is the difference between a retreat planner and a retreat facilitator?

A planner owns logistics: venue, contracts, travel and schedule. A facilitator owns the sessions in the room. A full-service retreat designer owns both, plus the outcome. If the retreat has to change how a team works, someone has to be accountable for that outcome, not only for the event running on time.

How much does an executive retreat company cost?

Few firms publish prices. Legendary Retreats publishes turnkey ranges from $35,000 to $50,000 for a Focused retreat, $45,000 to $150,000 for Signature, and $75,000 to $500,000+ for Bespoke, for teams of roughly 5 to 12, with airfare usually excluded. Logistics planners such as Offsite charge per-person planning fees on top of venue and travel. Facilitators usually quote a fee for design and facilitation only.
