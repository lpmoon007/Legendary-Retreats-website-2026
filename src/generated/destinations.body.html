A few of our favorites

We could run a retreat almost anywhere. These are a few we love.

This isn't a catalog to pick from — the destination always serves your outcome, never the reverse. But people ask where we go, so here are a handful of places we keep coming back to, and why. Most of our work is across the United States, with select retreats in Central America and worldwide on request.

Two decades, coast to coast

We've run retreats in nearly every corner of the country.

From the Sierra to the Everglades, the high San Juans to the Alaska backcountry. Each pin is a team we've taken somewhere deliberate. Wherever you are, there's a legendary setting within reach.

The San Juan Mountains

The most rugged, remote high country in the lower 48 — 14,000-foot peaks, old mining roads, and alpine wilderness that delivers in every season. Whatever your team needs — grit, exposure, awe, or total disconnection — this one range can give it.
