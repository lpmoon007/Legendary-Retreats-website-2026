Moab · Utah

Executive leadership retreats in Moab, Utah.

Red-rock canyon country and some of the most demanding off-road terrain in North America — an environment built for teams that need to face real adversity together and come out trusting each other more.

Why Moab

When the terrain gets hard, the team gets honest.

Moab's slickrock and ledges don't care about your title. On an obstacle that demands a spotter, a driver, and total trust, the org chart disappears and the real team shows up. That's the point. We use the terrain to surface how your team actually communicates under pressure — then channel it toward your specific outcome.

This is a format, not the product. If Moab serves your function, we run it. If a quiet estate serves it better, we'll tell you.

What a Moab immersion is good for
Trust under pressure

Real consequences turn polite colleagues into a team that depends on each other.

Decisive communication

No room for ambiguity on the trail — habits that carry straight back to the boardroom.

Shared identity

A hard thing done together becomes the story the team tells for years.

Planning a retreat in Moab

The practical details.

When to go

March to May and September to October. Summer days regularly pass 100°F, so trail days start at dawn; winter is cold, quiet and uncrowded.

Getting there

Canyonlands Regional (CNY) is about 20 minutes from town with limited service. Grand Junction (GJT) is about 1 hour 45 minutes; Salt Lake City (SLC) about 4 hours. Private aircraft can use CNY.

How long

Three days is the sweet spot: one arrival evening, one full day on the terrain, one morning to turn it into decisions.

Where you stay

A riverside luxury ranch or a private lodge, so the hard day ends somewhere comfortable enough to keep talking.

A sample three-day shape

A starting point. Every retreat is built around your one outcome.

Day 1

Arrive

Fly in by afternoon. Sunset on the rim, a long dinner, and the one question the retreat is built around.

Day 2

The terrain

A guided day on slickrock and ledges, driver and spotter in every vehicle. Debrief in the field, then again at dinner.

Day 3

Decide

A morning session that turns what the trail surfaced into commitments. Each leader leaves with one behavior; the 30 days begin.

What it costs

Every retreat here follows our three tiers: Focused $35,000–$50,000, Signature $45,000–$150,000 and Bespoke $75,000 and up, turnkey and exclusive of air travel. What moves the number here: spring and fall demand for lodging, private guides and vehicles, and whether you charter into Canyonlands. See what an executive retreat costs and the three tiers.

Straight answers

When is the best time of year for a retreat in Moab?

Spring (March to May) and fall (September to October). Summer is very hot, so any terrain day starts early; winter is quiet and cold but workable for the right format.

How do executives get to Moab?

Fly into Canyonlands Regional (CNY), about 20 minutes from town, or into Grand Junction (about 1 hour 45 minutes) or Salt Lake City (about 4 hours) and transfer by road. Private aircraft can land at CNY.

Does the team need off-road experience?

No. Guides brief every driver, then let the team read most obstacles and spot for each other, because working the terrain out together is part of the learning. Whenever a situation turns dangerous, a guide steps in and spots.

How long should a Moab retreat be?

Three days suits most executive teams: arrival evening, a full day on the terrain and a morning to turn the experience into decisions.

Can you run a retreat in Moab for a leadership team of 8 to 12?

Yes. Retreats in Moab are private and designed for leadership teams of roughly 5 to 12. Where you stay: a riverside luxury ranch or a private lodge, so the hard day ends somewhere comfortable enough to keep talking. Larger groups are possible with a different format; we will tell you honestly what changes.

What is included in a retreat in Moab?

Design, facilitation by James Carter, lodging, meals, activities, on-site coordination and ground transfers, plus a 30-day challenge with a written day-30 report. Airfare is the usual exclusion. Focused and Signature retreats come with a written proposal within 72 hours.

Who a retreat in Moab is for

Private retreats for CEOs and leadership teams of roughly 5 to 12, built around one outcome. We are strongest with teams that need to work as one: teams in conflict, split into camps, or newly merged. Every retreat is turnkey, and Focused and Signature retreats come with a written proposal within 72 hours.
