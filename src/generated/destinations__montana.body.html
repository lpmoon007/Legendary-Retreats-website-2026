Montana · Guest ranches, Big Sky & Yellowstone

Executive retreats & offsites in Montana.

Blue-ribbon rivers, working ranches, and wilderness that runs to the horizon — paired with five-star lodges. The place for a leadership team that wants to truly disconnect without giving anything up.

Why Montana

Wilderness that humbles. Comfort that keeps them talking.

Montana is the rare place that offers genuine remoteness and genuine refinement at once. A team can fly-fish a river, ride into the backcountry, or sit by a fire for a long, honest conversation — then sleep somewhere extraordinary. That combination is exactly why it suits a senior team that needs to drop its armor but won't tolerate roughing it.

Most Montana retreats are based at a five-star guest ranch, often bought out for the team. Paradise Valley runs south from Livingston to Gardiner and Yellowstone’s North Entrance, so a guided day in the park can be part of the design, in any season.

It fits flagship bespoke retreats, leadership-team realignment, and offsites built around real disconnection and shared experience. As always, this is a format, not the product — if Montana serves your outcome, we run it; if somewhere else serves it better, we'll tell you.

Where we take teams in Montana
Greenough · near Missoula

The Resort at Paws Up

A working ranch of about 37,000 acres on the Blackfoot River, roughly 35 minutes from Missoula International (MSO). Private cabins or glamping camps of safari-style tents, and days that run from fly-fishing, horseback riding and a cattle drive to rafting and rappelling, with dogsledding in winter.

Philipsburg · Rock Creek

The Ranch at Rock Creek

About 6,600 acres with roughly four miles of the blue-ribbon Rock Creek, about 90 minutes from Missoula. A Relais & Châteaux member and the first Forbes Five-Star ranch resort, with only 29 cabins, homes and creek-side tents: intimate enough to feel like the team’s own. Riding, fly-fishing, hiking and archery, with skiing nearby in winter.

What a Montana retreat or offsite is good for
Genuine remoteness

Far enough off-grid that the team is fully present — no exits, no distractions.

Wilderness + five-star

Rivers and backcountry by day, extraordinary lodges by night.

Shared experience that bonds

Fly-fishing, riding, and fireside candor become the story the team tells for years.

Planning a retreat in Montana

The practical details.

When to go

June to September for rivers, riding and ranch life; December to March at ski and winter-ranch properties.

Getting there

Bozeman (BZN) is about an hour from Big Sky and Paradise Valley; Missoula (MSO) is about 35 minutes from Paws Up and about 90 minutes from The Ranch at Rock Creek; Kalispell (FCA) serves northwest Montana. Remote ranches can be one to three hours out.

How long

Three to four days. Distance is part of the disconnection.

Where you stay

The Resort at Paws Up, The Ranch at Rock Creek, or another five-star guest ranch, often bought out for the team.

A sample three-day shape

A starting point. Every retreat is built around your one outcome.

Day 1

Arrive

Into Missoula or Bozeman, transfer to the ranch, and a first evening by the fire.

Day 2

The land

A morning of real work; an afternoon fly-fishing, riding or in the backcountry.

Day 3

Decide

A focused session to land decisions and one behavior per leader, then out.

What it costs

Every retreat here follows our three tiers: Focused $35,000–$50,000, Signature $45,000–$150,000 and Bespoke $75,000 and up, turnkey and exclusive of air travel. What moves the number here: ranch buy-outs, peak summer demand and transfer distance. See what an executive retreat costs and the three tiers.

Straight answers

Where do you run executive retreats in Montana?

Most often at two five-star ranches: The Resort at Paws Up in Greenough, about 37,000 acres on the Blackfoot River roughly 35 minutes from Missoula International, and The Ranch at Rock Creek near Philipsburg, a Relais & Châteaux ranch about 90 minutes from Missoula. We also build retreats around Big Sky and Paradise Valley, where Yellowstone is within reach.

When is the best time for a Montana retreat?

June to September for the classic ranch and river experience; winter for ski and snow-ranch formats.

Which airport do we use for a Montana retreat?

Missoula (MSO) for Paws Up, about 35 minutes away, and for The Ranch at Rock Creek, about 90 minutes; Bozeman (BZN) for Big Sky and Paradise Valley; Kalispell (FCA) for northwest Montana.

Do we have to rough it?

No. Montana pairs real remoteness with five-star guest ranches and lodges.

How long should a Montana retreat be?

Three to four days.

Can you run a retreat in Montana for a leadership team of 8 to 12?

Yes. Retreats in Montana are private and designed for leadership teams of roughly 5 to 12. Where you stay: a five-star guest ranch or private lodge, often bought out for the team. Larger groups are possible with a different format; we will tell you honestly what changes.

What is included in a retreat in Montana?

Design, facilitation by James Carter, lodging, meals, activities, on-site coordination and ground transfers, plus a 30-day challenge with a written day-30 report. Airfare is the usual exclusion. Focused and Signature retreats come with a written proposal within 72 hours.

Can a Montana retreat include Yellowstone?

Yes. Paradise Valley runs south to Gardiner and Yellowstone’s North Entrance, about 90 minutes from Bozeman, and the road into the park from Gardiner is open year-round. A guided day in the park can be built into the retreat when it serves the outcome.

Can we book a Montana guest ranch for an executive retreat?

Yes. Most Montana retreats are based at a five-star guest ranch or private lodge, often bought out for the team, so the whole property is private for the leadership team.

Who a retreat in Montana is for

Private retreats for CEOs and leadership teams of roughly 5 to 12, built around one outcome. We are strongest with teams that need to work as one: teams in conflict, split into camps, or newly merged. Every retreat is turnkey, and Focused and Signature retreats come with a written proposal within 72 hours.
