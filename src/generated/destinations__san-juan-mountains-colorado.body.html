San Juan Mountains · Colorado

Executive leadership retreats in the San Juan Mountains, Colorado.

The most rugged, remote high country in the lower 48 — a wall of 14,000-foot peaks, old mining roads, and alpine wilderness that delivers in every season. Few places on earth give a team more to work with: grit, exposure, beauty, and total disconnection from the office.

Why the San Juans

Everything you could possibly want from terrain — in one range.

The San Juans are Colorado at its most uncompromising: 14er summits, via ferrata, backcountry passes, historic mining camps, deep snow in winter and wildflower basins in summer. That range is the point — whatever your team's outcome demands, this one region can deliver it, from genuine adversity to awe. And it's remote enough that the office truly disappears.

This is a region, not a product. If the San Juans serve your function, few places rival them. If a quiet estate serves it better, we'll tell you that instead.

Trust under pressure

Real consequences turn polite colleagues into a team that depends on each other.

Decisive communication

No room for ambiguity at altitude — clear, calm communication that carries back to the boardroom.

Planning a retreat in the San Juans

The practical details.

When to go

July to September for high passes, via ferrata and wildflower basins. December to March for snow and winter-survival formats. Spring and late fall are shoulder seasons with closed passes.

Getting there

Montrose (MTJ) is about 45 minutes from Ouray and 1.5 hours from Telluride; Durango (DRO) is about 1 hour 15 minutes from Silverton. Telluride (TEX) handles private aircraft.

How long

Three to four days. The terrain and altitude reward a day to settle in.

Where you stay

Historic mountain towns and private lodges, from refined to deliberately remote, depending on the format.

A sample three-day shape

A starting point. Every retreat is built around your one outcome.

Day 1

Arrive and acclimate

Fly into Montrose or Durango, transfer into the mountains, and an easy first evening at altitude.

Day 2

The range

A guided day chosen for the outcome: a high pass by jeep, a via ferrata, or in winter a snow-shelter build.

Day 3

Decide

Turn the day into commitments, one behavior per leader, and travel out.

What it costs

Every retreat here follows our three tiers: Focused $35,000–$50,000, Signature $45,000–$150,000 and Bespoke $75,000 and up, turnkey and exclusive of air travel. What moves the number here: season (summer passes versus winter formats), private guiding, and transfers from Montrose or Durango. See what an executive retreat costs and the three tiers.

Straight answers

When is the best time to run a retreat in the San Juan Mountains?

July to September for the high country, December to March for snow formats. Many passes are closed in spring and late fall.

Which airport serves the San Juan Mountains?

Montrose (MTJ) for Ouray and Telluride, Durango (DRO) for Silverton, and Telluride (TEX) for private aircraft.

Is the altitude a problem for executives?

Towns sit around 7,700 to 9,300 feet. We plan an easy first evening, keep hydration and pacing in the design, and choose terrain to suit the team.

What results have come from the San Juans?

Our largest measured change, a 268% behavioral gain in 30 days, came from a winter-survival retreat in this range.

Could the San Juan Mountains be right for your team?

Start with the outcome you need. We'll tell you honestly whether the San Juans — or somewhere else — get you there.
