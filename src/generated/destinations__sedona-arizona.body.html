Sedona · Arizona

Executive retreats & offsites in Sedona, Arizona.

Red-rock canyon country, high-desert clarity, and some of the country's best resorts — a two-hour drive from Phoenix and a world away from the noise. The place teams come to step back, realign, and make the decisions that have been waiting.

Why Sedona

Step out of the noise, and the team can finally think.

Not every breakthrough needs an off-road trail or a snow cave. Some teams don't need more adversity — they need space, beauty, and a complete break from the inbox to do the hard thinking they keep deferring. Sedona delivers that: striking enough to reset the nervous system, comfortable enough to stay in the room for the conversation that matters.

It's a strong fit for strategy resets, realignment after a reorg or merger, and offsites where the work is candor and decisions — not survival. As always: this is a format, not the product. If Sedona serves your outcome, we run it. If somewhere else serves it better, we'll tell you.

What a Sedona retreat or offsite is good for
Strategic reset

Distance from the day-to-day so the team can lift its head and choose the next chapter on purpose.

Realignment after change

A neutral, restorative setting to rebuild trust and shared direction after a reorg, merger, or hard year.

Clarity & decisions

Room to have the candid conversation and leave with decisions made — not another deck of open questions.

Planning a retreat in Sedona

The practical details.

When to go

March to May and October to November are ideal. Summer is hot, with afternoon monsoon storms from July to September; winter is mild with the occasional snow.

Getting there

Phoenix Sky Harbor (PHX) is about 2 hours by road; Flagstaff (FLG) about 45 minutes. Sedona has a small airport for private aircraft.

How long

Two to three days. Sedona is built for thinking time, so the agenda leaves room to breathe.

Where you stay

One of the red-rock resorts, with private meeting space and terraces built for long conversations.

A sample three-day shape

A starting point. Every retreat is built around your one outcome.

Day 1

Arrive

Transfer from Phoenix, settle in, and an evening framing the one outcome over dinner.

Day 2

Think

Morning strategy work while the team is fresh; an afternoon on the red rock, on foot or off-road, chosen for the outcome.

Day 3

Decide

Decisions, owners and one behavior per leader. Back to Phoenix by mid-afternoon.

What it costs

Every retreat here follows our three tiers: Focused $35,000–$50,000, Signature $45,000–$150,000 and Bespoke $75,000 and up, turnkey and exclusive of air travel. What moves the number here: peak-season resort rates in spring and fall, and transfers from Phoenix. See what an executive retreat costs and the three tiers.

Straight answers

When is the best time for a Sedona retreat?

Spring (March to May) and fall (October to November). Summer brings heat and afternoon monsoon storms; winter is mild and quieter.

How far is Sedona from the airport?

About two hours by road from Phoenix Sky Harbor, or about 45 minutes from Flagstaff. Small private aircraft can use Sedona's own airport.

Is Sedona only for relaxed retreats?

No. It suits strategy resets and realignment, and the red-rock terrain also supports an off-road day when the outcome calls for shared challenge.

How long should a Sedona offsite be?

Two to three days for most leadership teams.

Can you run a retreat in Sedona for a leadership team of 8 to 12?

Yes. Retreats in Sedona are private and designed for leadership teams of roughly 5 to 12. Where you stay: one of the red-rock resorts, with private meeting space and terraces built for long conversations. Larger groups are possible with a different format; we will tell you honestly what changes.

What is included in a retreat in Sedona?

Design, facilitation by James Carter, lodging, meals, activities, on-site coordination and ground transfers, plus a 30-day challenge with a written day-30 report. Airfare is the usual exclusion. Focused and Signature retreats come with a written proposal within 72 hours.

Who a retreat in Sedona is for

Private retreats for CEOs and leadership teams of roughly 5 to 12, built around one outcome. We are strongest with teams that need to work as one: teams in conflict, split into camps, or newly merged. Every retreat is turnkey, and Focused and Signature retreats come with a written proposal within 72 hours.
