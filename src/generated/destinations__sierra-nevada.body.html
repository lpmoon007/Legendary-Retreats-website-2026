Sierra Nevada · California

Executive leadership retreats in the Sierra Nevada.

One range, every season — alpine winter survival, granite summer trails, and five-star mountain lodges. The widest range we offer, so the format can swing from grit to serenity depending entirely on what your team needs.

Why the Sierra Nevada

One place that can be exactly as hard, or as restorative, as the outcome demands.

The Sierra's gift is range. We've built snow-cave winter-survival immersions on its ridgelines and quiet, reflective strategy retreats in five-star mountain lodges a few miles away. Same range, opposite emotional registers — chosen deliberately, because the environment is always in service of the outcome.

Whether your team needs to be tested or to finally exhale, the Sierra can do either — by design, never by default.

What a Sierra Nevada retreat is good for
Range by design

From winter survival to lakeside reflection — one place that flexes to your outcome.

Reset & clarity

A change of altitude and pace that lets a team finally think clearly together.

Shared identity

A hard thing done together becomes the story the team tells for years.

Planning a retreat in the Sierra Nevada

The practical details.

When to go

June to October for granite trails, high lakes and the Rubicon; December to March for snow and winter-survival formats.

Getting there

Reno-Tahoe (RNO) and Sacramento (SMF) serve the northern Sierra; Fresno (FAT) the Yosemite side. Truckee (TRK) handles private aircraft.

How long

Three days for most formats; four when the team needs both a hard day and a reflective one.

Where you stay

Five-star mountain lodges a short drive from the backcountry, or a remote cabin when closeness is the point.

A sample three-day shape

A starting point. Every retreat is built around your one outcome.

Day 1

Arrive

Transfer from Reno or Sacramento and an evening framing the outcome.

Day 2

The range

Winter: a guided snow-shelter or ridge day. Summer: a granite trail or the Rubicon by four-wheel drive.

Day 3

Decide

A morning in the lodge to turn the day into decisions and one behavior per leader.

What it costs

Every retreat here follows our three tiers: Focused $35,000–$50,000, Signature $45,000–$150,000 and Bespoke $75,000 and up, turnkey and exclusive of air travel. What moves the number here: season, the remoteness of the format, and lodge rates near Tahoe and Yosemite. See what an executive retreat costs and the three tiers.

Straight answers

When is the best season for a Sierra Nevada retreat?

June to October for summer terrain, December to March for snow formats.

How do we get to the Sierra Nevada?

Reno-Tahoe or Sacramento for the northern Sierra, Fresno for the Yosemite side; Truckee for private aircraft.

Can the same retreat be both demanding and restful?

Yes. The Sierra lets us pair a hard day in the backcountry with reflective work in a five-star lodge a few miles away.

Do we need mountaineering experience?

No. Every day is guided and designed around the team you have.

Could the Sierra Nevada be right for your team?

Start with the outcome you need. We'll tell you honestly whether the Sierra — or somewhere else — gets you there.
