Luxury executive retreats near national parks.

Scale changes a conversation. These are the places we take leadership teams where a national park is part of the retreat, with five-star or historic lodging so the day outdoors ends in comfort.

By James Carter · Updated October 2026

Quick picks

  • Most dramatic setting with the easiest flight: Jackson Hole (the airport is inside Grand Teton National Park).
  • Ranch luxury and a Yellowstone day: Montana.
  • Silence, scale and a team that needs to depend on each other: Death Valley, November to March.
  • Red rock and off-road days between two parks: Moab.
  • Genuinely far from the office: Costa Rica, rafting into Pacuare and on to Tortuguero.

The destinations

Jackson Hole, Wyoming Retreats in Jackson Hole →

Nearby: Grand Teton and Yellowstone

The retreat
Teton ranch lodges with Grand Teton National Park on the doorstep; Jackson Hole Airport sits inside the park. In summer, Yellowstone's South Entrance is roughly an hour and a quarter north.
Best season
June to September; December to March

Montana Retreats in Montana →

Nearby: Yellowstone

The retreat
Luxury guest ranches such as The Resort at Paws Up and The Ranch at Rock Creek, or Big Sky. Paradise Valley runs south from Livingston to Yellowstone's North Entrance, so a guided park day can be built in.
Best season
June to September; December to March

Death Valley, California Retreats in Death Valley →

Nearby: Death Valley

The retreat
The historic Furnace Creek Inn inside the park, off-road days through Titus Canyon and Lippincott Pass, and a Gold Tier International Dark Sky Park overhead.
Best season
November to March only

Moab, Utah Retreats in Moab →

Nearby: Arches and Canyonlands

The retreat
Red-rock canyon country between two national parks, with off-road routes from town. Canyonlands Regional airport is about 20 minutes away.
Best season
March to May; September to October

Sedona, Arizona Retreats in Sedona →

Nearby: Grand Canyon (day trip)

The retreat
Resort retreats among the red rocks, with an off-road expedition day that can run from Sedona to the Grand Canyon.
Best season
March to May; October to November

Costa Rica Retreats in Costa Rica →

Nearby: Tortuguero

The retreat
Raft the Pacuare River into Pacuare Lodge in the rainforest, then cross to Tortuga Lodge on the canals of Tortuguero.
Best season
Year-round; turtles nest roughly July to October

Sierra Nevada, California Retreats in Sierra Nevada →

Nearby: Yosemite side

The retreat
Granite high country, the Rubicon Trail and winter-survival formats; Fresno serves the Yosemite side of the range.
Best season
June to October; December to March

Utah Retreats in Utah →

Nearby: Zion and the canyon parks

The retreat
Moab and Canyonlands for off-road days, Park City lodges, or Zion and St. George in the south on request.
Best season
March to May; September to October

What to plan for near a national park

Group permits and commercial use rules apply inside most parks, so guided activities usually run through a licensed operator. Peak-season lodging near the famous parks books up many months ahead, and a few lodges close in the shoulder seasons. Build the park into the design for a reason: a day in that landscape works best when it is tied to the conversation the team needs to have, not added as sightseeing.

Questions buyers ask

Where can we host a luxury corporate retreat near a national park?

Strong options include Jackson Hole (Grand Teton and Yellowstone), Montana guest ranches (Yellowstone), Death Valley (the historic Furnace Creek Inn inside the park), Moab (Arches and Canyonlands), Sedona (a Grand Canyon day), Costa Rica (Tortuguero) and the Sierra Nevada (the Yosemite side).

What is the best national park for an executive retreat?

For access and luxury, Jackson Hole, where the airport sits inside Grand Teton National Park. For a team that needs to depend on each other, Death Valley in winter. The right answer depends on the outcome the retreat has to deliver.

Do you need a permit to hold a corporate event in a national park?

Often, yes. Commercial and group activities inside national parks generally require permits or a licensed operator. A retreat company that works in the park should handle this.

When should we book a retreat near a national park?

Six to twelve months ahead for peak summer near Grand Teton, Yellowstone and the Utah parks. Death Valley runs only from November to March.
