What it is

A two-to-five-day retreat for the CEO and leadership team, designed and facilitated by James Carter. It starts from one question: if this retreat could achieve only one thing, what would it be? Every venue, activity and session is chosen to serve that answer.

Why it holds

Each leader leaves with one behavior tied to the outcome. For 30 days they get a morning reminder and a 4 p.m. check-in, and the team receives a behavioral-change report at the end. That follow-through is what most offsites lack.

Measured results

268%behavioral gain in 30 days, a team split into two camps
209%mindset lift, Champion Iron executive team, AlUla
178%teaming gain in 30 days, five newly hired leaders

"James helped us turn seven groups of rivals into one leadership team." Ronn Page, former CEO, Eagle Manufacturing Group

Three ways to run it

  • Focused, $35K–$50K: a ready agenda around one focus area at a luxury venue.
  • Signature, $45K–$150K: a proven venue and arc, content tailored to the outcome.
  • Bespoke, $75K–$500K+: designed from scratch around one measured outcome.

Turnkey: design, facilitation, lodging, meals, activities, on-site coordination, transfers and the 30-day reinforcement. Air travel excluded.

Who leads it

James Carter: 25 years inside leadership teams, more than 250 engineered retreats and days across 40 states and abroad. Co-author alongside Stephen Covey, Ken Blanchard and Deepak Chopra; featured by CNN and Business Insider. Leadership teams at American Express, Johnson & Johnson, Caterpillar, Bayer and Siemens.

Next step

A 15-minute call with James to agree the outcome. You leave with a format, a destination and a firm number, whether or not you proceed. Book at legendary-retreats.com or call +1-800-513-8759.
