Built on our published prices, not a sales trick.

Your need sets the tier

Straight answers

How accurate is this estimate?

It places your retreat inside our published tier ranges based on team size, length, setting and season. A firm proposal follows a 15-minute call, once we know the outcome, the dates and the place.

What is included in the estimate?

Design, facilitation by James Carter, lodging, meals, activities, on-site coordination, ground transfers and the 30-day reinforcement with its behavioral-change report. Air travel to the destination is not included.

Why is there a range rather than one number?

Venue, dates and the exact format move the price. The range is honest about that until those are decided.

What about a one-day experience?

A single engineered day is an Executive Immersion, priced separately from a multi-day retreat.

Can I share the estimate with my CEO or finance team?

Yes. Copy the link, or create a one-page brief with the estimate already filled in, ready to forward or print.
