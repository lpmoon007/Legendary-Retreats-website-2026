What the 30 days actually looks like

A great retreat fades by the second Monday. This is what keeps it alive.

The reason most offsites don’t stick isn’t the offsite — it’s that nothing changes in the operating rhythm the moment everyone is back at their desk. So we change the rhythm. Every leader leaves with one behavior, and for thirty days we hold them to it.

01

One behavior

Each leader leaves with a single behavior tied to the retreat’s outcome — not ten, one. The narrower the commitment, the more likely it survives contact with a busy week.

02

A morning nudge

Every morning, a reminder of the one thing you committed to. It starts the day pointed the right way, before the inbox decides your priorities for you.

03

A 4 p.m. check-in

Each afternoon, a check: did you do it — and a line about how it went. Said out loud, to someone, a commitment is far harder to quietly drop.

04

No app, no perfection

Nothing to download. You don’t have to be perfect. You have to keep showing up and doing your best — which, thirty days running, is what turns a resolution into how you work.

Thirty days is long enough for a behavior to stop being a resolution and start being how you work — and that, not the two days away, is what makes the retreat worth it.

Before you choose

Straight answers

Want a number for your team? Try the cost estimator. Approving a vendor? See the procurement kit.

What is the difference between Focused, Signature and Bespoke?

How much is designed from scratch. Focused uses a ready agenda around one of four focus areas at a luxury venue. Signature uses a proven venue and arc of activities with the content tailored to your outcome. Bespoke engineers everything, from the environment to the facilitation, around your team and one measured outcome.

What is included in the price?

Every tier is turnkey: design, facilitation by James Carter, lodging, meals, activities, on-site coordination, transfers and the 30-day reinforcement with its behavioral-change report. Air travel to the destination is the usual exclusion.

Is the 30-day reinforcement included in every tier?

Yes. Every leader leaves with one behavior tied to the outcome, gets a morning reminder and a 4 p.m. check-in for 30 days, and the team receives a behavioral-change report at the end.

How many people can attend?

The retreats are designed for executive and leadership teams of roughly 5 to 12. If your group is larger, ask: the format changes, and we will tell you honestly what will and won't work.

How far ahead should we book?

Three to six months gives you the widest choice of venues and dates. A Focused retreat can come together faster because the agenda is ready-built; a Bespoke retreat needs the longest runway, including pre-work sessions before you travel.

How do we choose the right tier?

Start from the outcome, not the budget. If the team is healthy and needs focused time on one topic, Focused is enough. If you need a real shift in how the team works, Signature. If the stakes are high and specific, Bespoke. The first 15-minute call ends with an honest recommendation, and sometimes it is the simpler option.
